Clerk has released native mobile SDK components for managing Organizations on iOS and Android platforms. These prebuilt UI views enable account switching and Organization settings functionality, with three main components: OrganizationSwitcher, OrganizationListView, and OrganizationProfileView. The components provide features ranging from account selection to membership management and Organization administration.
